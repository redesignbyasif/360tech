FAQ

Questions, answered

Do you offer 24-hour breakdown assistance?

Yes. Our commercial refrigeration service includes 24-hour breakdown assistance, so a critical failure doesn't have to mean extended downtime.

Do you work for both homes and businesses?

Absolutely. We serve private individuals, SMEs and multinationals alike — from a single air conditioning unit in a home to full installations across commercial premises.

Are you certified for refrigeration work?

Yes — we are F-Gas certified for refrigeration work, ensuring all installations and servicing are carried out in line with the required regulations.

Do you install heat pumps?

Yes. We are leaders in providing LG air-to-water heat pumps to suit home heating needs, installed and serviced by our qualified team.

Where are you based and what areas do you cover?

We are based at Unit 4, Zone C, Mullingar Industrial Estate, Mullingar, Co. Westmeath, and service home owners and businesses nationwide across Ireland.
